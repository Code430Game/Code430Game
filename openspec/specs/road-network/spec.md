# road-network Specification

## Purpose
The city's roads as data and as geometry: the OSM import into a validated road graph with classes, lanes, one-way streets, bridges, tunnels and layers, junctions that meet cleanly, road surfaces with their detail texture, and the validation report that finds the import's mistakes.
## Requirements
### Requirement: OSM Road Import
The import tool SHALL produce a road graph from OpenStreetMap data for a given bounding box, preserving connectivity, one-way direction, road class, lanes, bridges, tunnels, and layers, and SHALL keep `service=parking_aisle` and `service=driveway` ways as edges of class `parking` connected to the streets they meet.

#### Scenario: Import a bounding box
- **WHEN** the tool runs with a bounding box
- **THEN** it writes `road_graph.v0.json` that validates against the published schema
- **AND** every edge references the OSM way it came from

#### Scenario: One-way preserved
- **WHEN** an OSM way is tagged `oneway=yes`
- **THEN** the corresponding edge is one-way in the same direction

#### Scenario: Parking aisle kept
- **WHEN** a `highway=service` way is tagged `service=parking_aisle`
- **THEN** it is exported as an edge of class `parking` sharing nodes with the street it joins, and the validation report counts it under that class, not among real-street dead ends

### Requirement: Deterministic Output
Given identical input data and configuration, the import tool SHALL produce byte-identical output.

#### Scenario: Repeat run
- **WHEN** the tool runs twice on the same cached data
- **THEN** both outputs are identical

### Requirement: Default Value Transparency
When a road attribute is missing in OSM, the tool SHALL fill it from a configurable per-class default and SHALL record that the value was defaulted.

#### Scenario: Missing lane count
- **WHEN** a `primary` road has no `lanes` tag
- **THEN** the edge uses the configured default for `primary`
- **AND** `lanes` appears in the edge's `defaulted_fields`

### Requirement: Layer-Separated Junctions
Junction clustering SHALL NOT merge nodes that belong to different layers.

#### Scenario: Highway over a street
- **WHEN** a motorway bridge (layer 1) passes over a street (layer 0)
- **THEN** no junction connects them
- **AND** the bridge edge's elevation clears the street by at least the configured clearance

### Requirement: Road Network Validation Report
The tool SHALL produce a validation report listing disconnected components, classified dead ends, disconnected ramps, and one-way traps.

#### Scenario: Ramp that leads nowhere
- **WHEN** a `motorway_link` edge does not connect a motorway to another road
- **THEN** the report lists it with its OSM way id

### Requirement: Drivable Generated Roads
The game SHALL generate road, junction, and terrain meshes with collision from the road graph, split into 256 m chunks with LODs. Where the importer has merged nearby junction nodes into one, each road SHALL keep its own straight line through the merged junction instead of bending to the junction centre, so parallel carriageways stay parallel in the meshes, the lane paths and the map.

#### Scenario: Drive across a chunk seam
- **WHEN** the placeholder car drives from one chunk to the next
- **THEN** there is no gap, step, or missing collision at the seam

#### Scenario: Deck performance
- **WHEN** the testbed runs on the `deck` preset on a Steam Deck
- **THEN** frame time stays at or under 33.3 ms while driving through the district

#### Scenario: Divided road through a merged junction
- **WHEN** two one-way carriageways of the same street pass through a merged junction node
- **THEN** their ribbons, edge lines and minimap lines run parallel through it without crossing, and traffic on either carriageway continues straight without steering toward the junction centre

### Requirement: Map Data Attribution
Every map pack generated by the tool SHALL include OpenStreetMap (ODbL) and elevation-source attribution in its manifest.

#### Scenario: Pack manifest
- **WHEN** the Ottawa starter map pack is generated
- **THEN** its `pack.json` contains "© OpenStreetMap contributors" and the elevation source credit

### Requirement: Forks And Merges
Where a narrower road leaves or joins a through road at a shallow angle, the importer SHALL move the link's end to the through road's outer edge, fading the move out along the link, and SHALL tag the node as a fork or merge. The game SHALL draw no junction polygon there, SHALL taper the through road's width across the node, and SHALL NOT treat the node as a junction for traffic priority.

#### Scenario: Off-ramp
- **WHEN** a motorway link leaves a motorway at a node where the motorway continues straight
- **THEN** the link's ribbon starts on the motorway's outer lane and peels away from its edge, no junction polygon covers the motorway, and traffic on the motorway does not yield at the node

#### Scenario: Side street
- **WHEN** a road meets a through road at a steep angle
- **THEN** the node stays an ordinary junction with a polygon and trims

### Requirement: Bridge Structures
The pack SHALL describe each bridge (its grouped deck edges, span, level, kind and ends, from `bridge=*` ways and `man_made=bridge` outlines), and the game SHALL draw each bridge as a solid deck with an underside, fascia, supports clear of the roads beneath it, and abutments. Walkways that belong to a bridge SHALL be drawn on its deck at deck height, and no sidewalk SHALL join a road on another layer.

#### Scenario: Looking up from under an overpass
- **WHEN** the player stands on a street under a highway bridge and looks up
- **THEN** they see the deck's concrete underside, not the sky

#### Scenario: No pier in a lane
- **WHEN** a bridge spans a street
- **THEN** no pier stands within 1.5 m of that street's ribbon or its sidewalks

#### Scenario: Walkway on the deck
- **WHEN** a footway tagged as part of a bridge runs beside its deck
- **THEN** it is drawn at the deck's height with the parapet outside it
- **AND** the sidewalks of the road under the bridge stay on the ground

### Requirement: Layer Inputs From Files
Every importer layer that is fetched from Overpass SHALL also accept its payload from a local JSON file, so a generated or cached dataset builds a complete pack with no network access.

#### Scenario: Offline pack build
- **WHEN** the importer runs with `--osm-json` and a `--*-json` file for every enabled layer
- **THEN** it writes a complete pack without any Overpass request and the road graph's meta records each layer's file as its source
