## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Office Facades With A Base And A Top
Office and commercial buildings SHALL have a taller ground floor with an entrance on the street side, one of several facade types chosen per building, setbacks over a podium where the footprint allows, and roof structures, so neighbouring buildings differ.

#### Scenario: Downtown block
- **WHEN** the player looks along a downtown street
- **THEN** neighbouring office buildings show different facades, each with a visible entrance at street level
