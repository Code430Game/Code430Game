## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Unarmed Moves
With empty hands the player SHALL punch on fire and kick on a held-then-released fire, with poses on the body, and hits SHALL follow the tone: knock-downs in gritty and dystopia, shoves in clean.

#### Scenario: Punching in clean
- **WHEN** the player punches a walker three times in clean
- **THEN** the walker stumbles and runs off and is never knocked down

#### Scenario: Kicking in gritty
- **WHEN** the player kicks a walker in gritty
- **THEN** the walker is knocked down and the kick counts as an assault where it is witnessed
