## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Road Shoulders And Deck Infill
Motorway, trunk and link roads SHALL have a paved shoulder outside their outer lanes, on the ground and on bridges, with parapets at the shoulder edge. Gaps narrower than the infill limit enclosed between carriageways of one bridge structure SHALL be filled with a concrete floor at deck height instead of being walled holes.

#### Scenario: Looking up from under the 417
- **WHEN** the player stands on Metcalfe Street under the 417
- **THEN** the decks overhead read as solid, with no walled hole between the carriageways showing the sky

#### Scenario: Driving the motorway
- **WHEN** the player drives the 417
- **THEN** there is paved shoulder outside the outer lane before the parapet or verge
