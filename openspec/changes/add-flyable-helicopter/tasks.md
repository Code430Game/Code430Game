## 2. Flying
- [x] 2.1 `FlyableHelicopter`: arcade flight (stick moves with bank and pitch, climb and descend, turn, hold and level when let go, top speed, ceiling, soft district boundary), tunables in `helicopter_flight.json`; tests on the flight maths (holds when let go, top speed, ceiling, boundary)
  - 2026-10-07: a wheel-less `VehicleBody3D`, so the Player, chase camera, minimap, anchors and co-op treat it like a car. Velocity is set in `_integrate_forces` (gravity off while flying): the stick asks for a horizontal velocity in the heading frame (42 m/s top, 9 m/s² accel, 12 m/s² brake when let go), triggers for a climb of 10 or descent of 8 m/s (none pressed holds height), bumpers for a turn (1.4 rad/s). Tilt is looks only (14° max). Descent slows near the ground (height × 1.2 + 1 m/s). Ceiling 180 m above the ground below; soft boundary 80 m in from the map edge. The rotor spools in 2.5 s and lifts from 80 %. Tunables in `helicopter_flight.json`.
- [x] 2.2 `fly` controls context (pad and keyboard), Controls tab and `docs/controls.md` regenerated; get in at the pad (Y / E); get out only landed or within 2 m ("Land first" hint); tests
  - 2026-10-07: new actions `fly_up` (RT / Space), `fly_down` (LT / Ctrl), `fly_yaw_left` (LB / Left), `fly_yaw_right` (RB / Right); the stick, look, look behind, get out (Y / E), camera, radio, map and pause as when driving. Keyboard turning is on the arrows because Q is the radio and E is get out. The pause menu's Controls tab and `docs/controls.md` show a new "Flying (the helicopter)" context (`controls_doc.py` knows it and the arrow keys). `Player.try_exit_vehicle` asks a vehicle with `can_exit()`; a refusal emits `exit_refused("land_first")` and the HUD shows "Land first" for 2 s.
- [x] 2.3 Landing on flat solid surfaces (ground, decks, roofs); a parked stolen helicopter stays put; collisions by tone from `outcomes.aircraft` (damage and auto-rotate down to disabled in gritty and dystopia; bounce and gentle auto-land in clean); tests in every tone
  - 2026-10-07: a contact closing faster than 7 m/s is a hit (`impacted` fires). Clean (`outcomes.aircraft: bounce`) bounces and lands itself, then gives control back. Gritty and dystopia (`damage`) bounce and lose health ((speed − 3.5) × 6 per hit); at zero it is disabled, glides down at 9 m/s and stays a wreck you can get out of. A mode without violence never wrecks it. With nobody aboard it lands where it is and, once still for 1 s, freezes (parked). With no ground found below (not streamed in yet) it hovers rather than sinking. It lands on roofs (shot on Police HQ's roof). The tone schema gains `outcomes.aircraft` (v0, pre-release).
- [x] 2.4 Camera (aircraft chase mode), altitude on the HUD, streamer look-ahead by speed and height (capped on deck); tests on the anchor look-ahead
  - 2026-10-07: the chase camera takes an aircraft's `chase_distance_m` and `chase_height_m` (9 m back and 3.5 m up, plus 0.08 m per m/s) and `chase_smoothing` (14, so it keeps up). The HUD speed line adds "alt N m". `ChunkStreamer.look_ahead` adds a second anchor 8 s of travel plus 1 m per metre of height ahead, capped by the preset's new `aircraft_lookahead_m` (deck 300 m, high 600 m). Found on the way: a long jump (or flying fast) downgraded chunks with meshes and shapes still queued, and typing a freed node into a variable raised an error each frame (180 on one jump to the pad); `_create_queued_shapes` checks before typing now, with a test.
