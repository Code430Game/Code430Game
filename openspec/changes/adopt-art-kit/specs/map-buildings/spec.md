## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Kit Facades On Footprints
Buildings SHALL keep their real footprints and heights and be dressed with kit facade pieces: bays of windows and walls by floor, doors and storefronts at street level, corner pieces, and kit roofs or generated roofs on the kit palette.

#### Scenario: A downtown street
- **WHEN** the player looks along a downtown street after the buildings stage
- **THEN** buildings follow their real footprints and show kit facades with storefronts and entrances at street level
