## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Characters From The Art Kit
The player, remote players, pedestrians, officers, paramedics and gang members SHALL be assembled from kit body, head, hair and outfit parts on the kit skeleton, picked and coloured by the character profile, with the two body types clearly distinct and clothes recognisable as what they are.

#### Scenario: A downtown crowd
- **WHEN** the player walks a busy street after the people stage
- **THEN** pedestrians wear varied kit outfits (coats, jackets, dresses, shorts) and walk with the crowd shader as before
