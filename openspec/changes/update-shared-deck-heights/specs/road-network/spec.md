## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shared Height For Touching Carriageways
Carriageways of one bridge whose ribbons touch SHALL share one surface height across the span, blending into their approaches, so no step shows between them.

#### Scenario: The Mackenzie King Bridge
- **WHEN** the player drives across from one carriageway of the Mackenzie King Bridge to the other
- **THEN** there is no step or visible side face between them
