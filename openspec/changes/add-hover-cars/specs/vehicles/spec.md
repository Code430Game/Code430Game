## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Hover Cars In Dystopia
In the dystopia tone, hover cars SHALL drive on the road like cars, climb and descend on the climb controls up to a 60 m ceiling, hold their height off the controls, and collide with the world.

#### Scenario: Taking off
- **WHEN** the player drives a hover car in dystopia and holds climb
- **THEN** it rises, holds its height when the control is released, and stops at 60 m

#### Scenario: Other tones
- **WHEN** the tone is clean or gritty
- **THEN** no hover cars appear in traffic, parking or police
