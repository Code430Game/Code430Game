## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Garage Minimap And Clean Ramp Cuts
While the player is inside a garage, the minimap SHALL show that garage's floor, and ramp cuts SHALL leave no hole in the terrain beside the ramp walls.

#### Scenario: Driving down into the NAC garage
- **WHEN** the player drives down the NAC ramp onto the garage floor
- **THEN** the minimap shows the garage's stalls and ramps instead of the street above
