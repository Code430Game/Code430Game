## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Two-Wheelers
Bicycles and motorcycles SHALL be rideable with a lean model, takeable like cars, and SHALL throw their rider (as a ragdoll) when braking hard at speed or crashing.

#### Scenario: Taking a bicycle
- **WHEN** the player takes a cyclist's bicycle and rides it
- **THEN** it leans through turns and the player can ride it in the bike lane past stopped cars
