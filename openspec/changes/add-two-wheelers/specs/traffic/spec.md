## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Cyclists In Bike Lanes
Cyclists SHALL ride imported bike lanes and cycleways in traffic, obeying signals, and SHALL NOT block cars from using a bike lane to pass.

#### Scenario: A street with a painted bike lane
- **WHEN** the player drives a street whose bike lane is tagged in the pack
- **THEN** cyclists ride in it and the player can still drive into it
