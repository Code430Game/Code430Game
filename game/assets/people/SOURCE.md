# Kit people

Built by `tools/people_kit` (`scripts/build-people.sh`) from the Quaternius Ultimate Modular Men
and Women packs in `art/quaternius/modular_men` and `art/quaternius/modular_women` (CC0 1.0).
The kit parts (faces, hair, beards, hats, outfits) are moved onto the game's eleven-bone rig,
lightened and recoloured by role; `people.json` holds the rig pivots and the names of the
choices. License: CC0 1.0, like their source; credit to Quaternius is appreciated and given in
`LICENSE-ASSETS`.

Don't edit these files by hand: change `tools/people_kit/recipes.json` and rebuild.
