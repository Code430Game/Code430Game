# Kit vehicles

Built by `tools/vehicle_kit` (`scripts/build-vehicles.sh`) from the Quaternius Cars Pack in
`art/quaternius/cars` (CC0 1.0). Sedan, hatchback, coupe, SUV, taxi and police are the kit
models with lighter wheels; the ambulance, van, box truck and pickup are derived from the kit
SUV (its cab plus new bodies). License: CC0 1.0, like their source; credit to Quaternius is
appreciated and given in `LICENSE-ASSETS`.

Don't edit these files by hand: change `tools/vehicle_kit/recipes.json` and rebuild.
