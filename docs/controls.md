# Controls

The on-screen hints switch to whichever device you touched last. The same list is in the
game: the pause menu's **Controls** tab (LB / RB or Q / E change tabs) shows the layout for
the device in use, and A (or Enter) on an action rebinds it with the next press; a press
already used by another action at the same time is refused and names that action. The last
row puts every control back to the defaults. Rebinds are saved in `user://settings.cfg`.

R3 does two things while driving: a tap looks behind, and holding it for a second resets a
stuck car (a ring on the HUD fills while you hold). The debug buttons work while View / Back
is held; their keys work alone.

The tables below are generated from the input map; edit `game/core/input/controls.json` or
the bindings in `game/project.godot` and run `uv run python controls_doc.py` in `tools/`.

**Options and Settings.** The Options tab also sets the music volume; the Settings tab picks
the graphics preset (Auto, Deck or High; Auto picks Deck on a Steam Deck).

**Tone** on the pause menu's Options tab picks the tone mode with left and right: Clean, Gritty (default) or
Dystopia. Billboards along the big roads change their ads with it; in co-op the host's tone is
the session's. `--tone=clean` starts in a mode for the session only.

**Character** on the pause menu's Game tab opens the creator: up and down pick a row, left and right change
it, A randomises, loads, saves or deletes a preset, B keeps the body and returns. Rows: skin, hair style
and colour, top, jacket and its colour, trousers, shoes, hat, beard, glasses, height, build. The first start
gives you a random body; the last one you made is loaded next time. Saved in `user://characters.json`.

Co-op has its own tab in the pause menu: **Host co-op game** shows the address friends join at;
**Join game** uses the address field (right on the Join line focuses it for the Deck's
on-screen keyboard). Command-line equivalents: `--host[=port]` and `--join=address[:port]`.

Testbed flags worth knowing: `--time=HH` starts the clock at that hour (`--time=23` for night),
`--day-minutes=N` sets how many real minutes a day lasts (default 24), `--mute` silences audio,
`--bus-share=0.5` makes half of the spawned traffic buses (default 8 %).
For screenshots: `--camera=px,py,pz,tx,ty,tz` fixes the view, `--creator` opens the character
creator once the world is up, `--screenshot=path` saves a PNG when the benchmark ends.
`--radio=hilltop|redline|quest|crypt|chatter|off` tunes the car radio at start. `--record-music=path.wav` records the Music bus
until the benchmark ends.
`--menu=game|coop|controls|options|settings[,keyboard]` opens the pause menu on that tab once the world is up.
`--report` opens "Report a problem" once the world is up, and `--report-send` also saves (and,
with a relay configured, sends) the report.
